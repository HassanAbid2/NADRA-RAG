NADRA Fee Structure — Processing Fee and Timeline for CNIC and Smart CNIC (Inland, in PKR).
Every service has three priority categories: Normal, Urgent, and Executive.

New CNIC: Normal fee Rs. 0/- (free) processed in 15 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,150/- in 12 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,150/- in 6 days.
CNIC Modification: Normal fee Rs. 400/- in 15 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,150/- in 12 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,150/- in 6 days.
Duplicate CNIC: Normal fee Rs. 400/- in 15 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,150/- in 12 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,150/- in 6 days.
CNIC Renewal: Normal fee Rs. 400/- in 15 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,150/- in 12 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,150/- in 6 days.

New Smart NIC (Smart CNIC): Normal fee Rs. 750/- in 31 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,500/- in 23 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,500/- in 9 days.
Smart NIC Modification: Normal fee Rs. 750/- in 31 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,500/- in 23 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,500/- in 9 days.
Smart NIC Duplicate: Normal fee Rs. 750/- in 31 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,500/- in 23 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,500/- in 9 days.
Smart NIC Renewal: Normal fee Rs. 750/- in 31 days; Urgent fee Rs. 1,500/- in 23 days; Executive fee Rs. 2,500/- in 9 days.

NADRA Fee Structure — CRC, FRC, and Cancellation Certificate fees and timelines.

CNIC / CRC Cancellation (due to death): Normal fee Rs. 0/- (free) processed in 1 day. Urgent and Executive not applicable.

CRC (Child Registration Certificate, all categories): Normal fee PKR 50/- in 7 days; Executive fee PKR 500/- in 1 day. Urgent not applicable.

FRC (Family Registration Certificate) for Alpha / Beta / Gama family: Executive fee PKR 1,000/- in 1 day. Normal and Urgent not applicable.
FRC (Family Registration Certificate) for Both: Executive fee PKR 2,000/- in 1 day. Normal and Urgent not applicable.

NADRA Fee Structure — Age Modification in ID Cards, and Succession Certificate.

Processing Fee for Age Modification in ID Cards:
Age change up to 1 year: Inland Rs. 1,000/-, Abroad USD 15/-.
Age change more than 1 year and up to 2 years: Inland Rs. 2,000/-, Abroad USD 25/-.
Age change more than 2 years and up to 3 years: Inland Rs. 3,000/-, Abroad USD 40/-.
Age change more than 3 years: Inland Rs. 5,000/-, Abroad USD 65/-.
Second time age change: Inland Rs. 10,000/-, Abroad USD 125/-.

Processing Fee for issuance of Succession Certificate (and decline on processed application):
New / Fresh application, asset worth equal to or more than Rs. 100,000/-: Rs. 20,000/- (Rs. 22,000/- in case of Sindh). Asset worth less than Rs. 100,000/-: Rs. 10,000/-.
Duplicate Application: Rs. 5,000/- (both asset brackets).
Amended Application: Rs. 5,000/- (both asset brackets).
Decline on Processed application: Rs. 15,000/- (both asset brackets).
Office Mistake: Free of Cost.

NADRA Fee Structure — Delivery Charges, Validity Periods, Important Notices, and Fee Deposit Bank Details.

Delivery Charges (ID Cards):
Abroad Delivery: Varies from country to country.
Within Pakistan: PKR 165/-.

Validity Period of ID Cards and Certificates:
CNIC, Smart CNIC / NICOP for Adults: 10 years.
Smart NICOP for minors: 10 years.
Smart NICOP for minors above 14 years of age: 10 years.
Smart NICOP for minors below 14 years of age: 10 years or 18 years (whichever comes first).
Juvenile Card: valid upon attaining the age of 10 and 18 years.
CRC: valid upon attaining the age of 3 or 10 or 18 years.
Smart POC (Ex-Pakistani, Parents based, FPO family members based): 7 years.
Smart POC (Spouse based): 5 years.
